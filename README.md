# 🛍️ Consumer Behavior Analysis Using AI Techniques

### Understanding Shopping Channels, Product Preferences, Consumer Concerns & Lifestyle Segments

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes consumer behavior in the beauty and wellness sector using **Microsoft Excel, Power BI, and AI-assisted analytical techniques**. The goal is to understand shopping-channel preferences, product-category interests, skin and hair concerns, price preferences, and lifestyle segments.

The project uses a dataset of 100 consumer records to identify patterns, develop illustrative consumer personas, and suggest data-driven marketing and product-personalization strategies.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze consumer shopping-channel preferences.
- Identify popular beauty and wellness product categories.
- Understand common skin and hair concerns.
- Examine consumer price preferences and age distribution.
- Explore relationships between lifestyle segments and product categories.
- Develop illustrative consumer personas using AI-assisted interpretation.
- Build an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Recommend marketing and personalization strategies based on the findings.

## 🧰 Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** — Data cleaning, preparation, and exploratory analysis.
- **Microsoft Power BI** — Interactive dashboards, charts, KPIs, and data visualization.
- **AI-Assisted Analysis** — Pattern interpretation, persona development, and recommendation brainstorming.

## 📂 Dataset Description

The dataset contains **100 consumer records** with attributes related to beauty and wellness shopping behavior.

Key fields include:

- Consumer ID
- Age
- Location
- Lifestyle
- Main Concern
- Preferred Category
- Shopping Channel
- Price Preference
- Purchase Influence
- Consumer Persona

## 📊 Key Findings

- **Total Consumers:** 100
- **Leading Shopping Channel:** Offline Store — 21%
- **Leading Skin/Hair Concern:** Anti-aging
- **Leading Price Preference:** Value for Money
- **Highest-Ranked Product Categories:** Body Care and Men’s Grooming

These findings describe the supplied dataset and should not be treated as market-wide conclusions.

## 📈 Power BI Dashboard

The interactive dashboard includes:

- KPI cards for key consumer insights.
- A donut chart showing shopping-channel distribution.
- A bar chart ranking preferred product categories.
- A bar chart comparing skin and hair concerns.
- A bar chart showing price preferences.
- A column chart displaying age distribution.
- A matrix comparing lifestyle segments with product categories.
- Interactive filters for exploring available data.

## 🤖 AI-Assisted Consumer Personas

The project explores five illustrative personas:

1. **Value-Driven Offline Shopper** — Interested in in-store shopping and value for money.
2. **Social & Mobile Beauty Explorer** — Interested in social commerce and mobile product discovery.
3. **Concern-Focused Skincare Seeker** — Focused on specific skin or hair concerns.
4. **Lifestyle-Aligned Fitness Enthusiast** — Interested in products that fit an active lifestyle.
5. **Time-Pressed Professional** — Values convenient product discovery and simplified choices.

These personas are exploratory hypotheses, not statistically validated customer segments.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Develop an omnichannel shopping experience.
- Communicate product value and pricing clearly.
- Organize product information around consumer concerns.
- Test lifestyle-based marketing campaigns.
- Improve product discovery and category navigation.
- Use dashboards to monitor changing consumer preferences.
- Validate recommendations through actual campaign and purchase data.

## ⚙️ Methodology

1. Prepare and review the consumer dataset.
2. Conduct exploratory data analysis.
3. Analyze shopping channels, categories, concerns, and price preferences.
4. Compare lifestyle segments with product-category preferences.
5. Create interactive Power BI visualizations.
6. Use AI-assisted interpretation to develop hypotheses and personas.
7. Formulate recommendations and document limitations.

## ⚠️ Limitations

- The dataset contains only 100 consumer records.
- Recorded preferences may not reflect actual purchases.
- The sample may not represent the entire beauty and wellness market.
- Consumer personas require further validation.
- AI-assisted interpretation was used; a predictive machine-learning model was not trained.

## 🚀 Future Scope

- Analyze a larger consumer dataset.
- Include actual purchase and customer feedback data.
- Explore customer segmentation with validated statistical or machine-learning methods.
- Track changes in shopping preferences over time.
- Test personalized marketing strategies using measurable outcomes.
